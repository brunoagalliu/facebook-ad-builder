# Body Copy Frameworks

Structures for carrying a reader from the hook to a decision without losing momentum. Drawn from Halbert, Bencivenga, Carlton, and classic PAS/AIDA formula work. Pick the framework that fits the ad's format and length constraint — don't force a long narrative into a 125-character bullet ad.

## Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)
1. **Problem** — name it precisely, in the reader's own words, not the marketer's.
2. **Agitate** — make the cost of the problem vivid and immediate (what it's costing them right now, not someday).
3. **Solve** — introduce the offer as the direct resolution, with a reason it works and a reason to act today.
This is the default workhorse for short-form ad copy because it compresses well into 2-3 lines.

## Before-After-Bridge
State where the reader is now (before), paint where they could be (after), and the offer is the bridge between the two. Effective when the after-state is highly visual/specific — this pairs naturally with image-led creative.

## Star-Story-Solution (Bencivenga)
Open with a compelling character or situation (the "star" — can be the reader, a customer, or the product itself as protagonist), build a short story with tension, resolve it with the product. Best for longer-form or storytelling-style variations; too slow for a 125-character bullet ad.

## Bullet-driven benefit stacking
For short, scroll-stopping formats: 2-4 bullets, each one benefit, each one starting strong (a number, an outcome, a "no more X"). Bullets should escalate — strongest or most surprising benefit last, not first, so the reader's attention builds toward the CTA rather than peaking early.

## Choosing a framework
- Very short (bullet/social feed) → PAS compressed to one line each, or bullet-driven stacking.
- Longer-form or storytelling variation → Before-After-Bridge or Star-Story-Solution.
- Skeptical/sophisticated audience → PAS with the "solve" step leading on mechanism/proof rather than just the promise, since generic promises have already been discounted by this reader (see market-awareness guidance in `01_hooks_and_headlines.md`).

Regardless of framework, the last line before the CTA must reconnect to urgency (see `00_doctrine.md`) — a framework that ends on emotional resolution without a reason to act now has stopped one beat too early.
