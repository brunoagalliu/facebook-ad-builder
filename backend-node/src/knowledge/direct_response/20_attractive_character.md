# Attractive Character Archetypes

Drawn from Russell Brunson's *Expert Secrets*. Governs *who* is speaking in a first-person testimonial or story-driven variation (see `12_advertorial_and_native_craft.md` and `19_pastor_framework.md`) — the persona changes what kind of claims read as credible from that voice.

## The archetypes

- **The Reluctant Hero** — didn't set out to find this, discovered it by accident or necessity while dealing with the same problem the reader has now. Highly credible for skeptical audiences because the character isn't positioned as an expert selling something, just someone who stumbled onto a result.
- **The Reporter** — investigated or uncovered something on the reader's behalf and is delivering findings, not opinions ("I looked into why most joint supplements don't work, and here's what I found"). Borrows journalistic credibility rather than personal-experience credibility.
- **The Leader** — has already walked the path and is now guiding others who are where they used to be. Works when real expertise/authority is genuinely available to claim (see `03_psychological_triggers.md`'s authority trigger) — using this voice without real standing reads as presumptuous.

## Stay inside the identification match

Whichever archetype is used, the character's *starting point* should mirror the reader's actual situation as given in the CustomerProfile — a Reluctant Hero only works if their "before" state is genuinely the reader's current state (see `06_angle_and_identification.md`). An archetype whose journey starts somewhere the reader doesn't recognize breaks identification even if the archetype itself is well-executed.

## Practical rule for this engine

Pick one archetype per variation and stay consistent within it — a Reporter voice ("I investigated...") shouldn't shift into Leader voice ("let me teach you...") mid-copy, since each implies a different, incompatible source of credibility. Default to the Reluctant Hero for cold/skeptical audiences and the Leader only when the brand voice or product genuinely supports an authority claim.
