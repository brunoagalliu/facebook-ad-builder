# Psychological Triggers

The specific levers that make "act now" feel true rather than manufactured. Drawn from Cialdini's influence principles and Kennedy's direct-response applications of them. Use 1-2 per variation, deployed honestly — stacking every trigger into one ad reads as manipulative and erodes trust faster than using none.

- **Scarcity** — limited quantity, limited spots, limited time. Only works when true or at least plausible for the offer; fabricated scarcity that reads as fake (e.g. a permanently-running "only 3 left") trains the audience to distrust the whole ad.
- **Urgency** — a deadline distinct from scarcity: the offer, price, or bonus reverts after a specific point. Pair with a concrete date/countdown where the format allows it.
- **Social proof** — numbers, reviews, "join X others," visible results from people like the reader. Strongest when specific ("12,847 customers" beats "thousands") and when the people cited resemble the target audience.
- **Authority** — credentials, media mentions, expert endorsement, or a founder's specific expertise. Useful for skeptical/sophisticated markets that have been burned by generic promises before.
- **Reciprocity** — leading with something free or valuable (a guide, a sample, a diagnostic) before asking for the click, so the ask feels like a fair trade rather than a cold pitch.
- **Loss aversion** — framing the cost of inaction rather than only the benefit of action. Generally the strongest single trigger in this list; a reader is more moved by "stop losing X" than "start gaining X" for an equivalent outcome.
- **Identity/aspiration** — positioning the action as consistent with who the reader already believes they are or wants to be ("for people who refuse to settle for..."). Effective for higher-consideration or lifestyle-adjacent offers; weaker for pure commodity/price-driven offers.

## Selecting triggers by offer type
- Time-limited discount/deal → scarcity + urgency.
- New/unfamiliar product or mechanism → social proof + authority (build belief before asking for action).
- Commodity or price-sensitive offer → loss aversion + urgency (what they're currently overpaying/missing).
- Identity-driven or lifestyle offer → identity/aspiration + social proof (from people like them).

Whatever is chosen, it must still pass the `00_doctrine.md` test: the trigger has to point at *now*, not just at desire in the abstract.
